# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers in the middle of an active search. They are applying to multiple roles
at once, often under time pressure and some stress. Their context when using this is
"I have a job description in front of me and I need a tailored resume and cover letter
I can actually submit, and I need to believe what it produces about me is true."

The job to be done: turn a master resume plus a specific job description into tailored,
submittable documents, by generating many grounded variants and choosing the ones that
sound right. They are exercising taste over a pool of options, not authoring from blank.

## Product Purpose

A web app for the conjurer pipeline: upload (or reuse) a master resume, paste a job
description, generate grounded variants for each bullet and paragraph, compare and pick
the winners, and stitch them into a final resume and cover letter ready to export.

It exists because one-shot AI resume output is generic and unverifiable. The conjurer's
bet is the opposite: summon abundance, then recognize the right variant. Success is the
user reaching a stitched document they would submit without rewriting, and trusting that
every claim in it traces back to their own evidence.

## Brand Personality

Calm and editorial. Quiet, document-respecting, generous with whitespace; it feels like
a focused writing and reading tool, not a dashboard and not a chat box. Voice is plain,
honest, and specific. It earns confidence through restraint and readability rather than
enthusiasm. Three words: calm, grounded, exacting.

## Anti-references

All four are hard no-gos:

- **Generic AI-chat UI.** No purple gradients, sparkle icons, chat bubbles, or "AI-powered"
  framing. Nothing that reads as a ChatGPT wrapper.
- **Cluttered resume-builder.** No Zety / Canva / MyPerfectResume density: no template
  galleries, upsell banners, busy toolbars, or ad-like CTAs.
- **Loud SaaS landing bleeding into the app.** No big gradient hero metrics, glassmorphism
  cards, or marketing-speak buttons inside the working tool.
- **Sterile enterprise HR.** No Workday / ATS gray-on-gray corporate blandness. Functional
  is not enough; it must not feel emotionally dead.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the evidence, don't promise it.** Trust is the product. Every generated claim
   exposes its trace back to the master resume or evidence pool. Provenance is visible and
   one interaction away, never hidden behind a reassuring label.
2. **The document is the hero.** This is an editorial tool. Resume and cover-letter text
   gets the typographic care of a reading app; UI chrome stays subordinate to the words.
3. **Abundance, then taste.** The core mechanic is comparing variants and choosing. Make
   comparison effortless and the pick feel decisive; the interface's job is to sharpen
   recognition, not to decide.
4. **Calm under pressure.** Users are stressed and time-boxed. The interface lowers
   cognitive load: one clear task per screen, restraint over feature density.
5. **Honesty over polish.** When something cannot be grounded, say so plainly. Credibility
   comes from admitting limits, not from hiding them behind confident styling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the baseline, done properly: AA contrast on all text (including placeholders and
muted copy), full keyboard navigability, visible focus states, and a real
`prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation. Readable type sizes and line
lengths throughout. No named higher-tier or specialized requirements at this time.
