# Application preparation

## Language

**Application:** The job-specific outline, generated variants, selected drafts, and final documents prepared from career sources.

**Unit:** One cover-letter paragraph or resume bullet identified by an outline entry.

**Variant:** One proposed wording for a unit, with references to its supporting evidence.

**Pick:** The applicant's selected variant for a unit; recorded by the checkbox in the shared variant document.

**Failed unit:** An outline unit whose generation attempt did not produce a complete usable variant set. It remains part of the application and can be retried.
