# Application preparation

## Language

**Application:** The job-specific outline, generated variants, selected drafts, and final documents prepared from career sources.

**Unit:** One cover-letter paragraph or resume bullet identified by an outline entry.

**Variant:** One proposed wording for a unit, with references to its supporting evidence.

**Pick:** The applicant's selected variant for a unit; recorded by the checkbox in the shared variant document.

**Failed unit:** An outline unit whose generation attempt did not produce a complete usable variant set. It remains part of the application and can be retried.

**Grimoire:** The workspace's encoded taste: the voice, claim discipline, bullet and letter patterns, and glossary that every generated variant follows.

**Glossary:** The grimoire section that maps each internal term to the public description an outside reader understands, with optional aliases. It holds no numbers.
_Avoid_: translation table, jargon list.

**Internal term:** A name that only means something inside one employer, such as a service codename, internal API or tool name, team name, hostname, or release label. Public technologies (Kafka, Kubernetes, FastAPI) are not internal terms.
_Avoid_: jargon, codename (when the meaning is the broader class).

**Public description:** The glossary's wording for an internal term, written for an engineer or recruiter at another company: what the thing does plus an industry category.

**Scale fact:** A sourced number that gives an internal term its size (volume, users, teams). It lives in the master resume, in the bullet that names the term, so the claim check can cite it.

**Outside reader:** The audience every application is written for: a recruiter or hiring manager who has never worked at the applicant's employer.

**Glossary leak:** An internal term, or one of its aliases, appearing in a variant or a final document.
