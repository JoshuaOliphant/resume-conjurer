# Variant-generation eval journal — 2026-10-06

La Boeuf asked for an eval of variant generation to decide a model migration off `claude-sonnet-4-6`, graded with TypeSafe Jev. Work is on `worktree-variant-generation-eval`. Cases are 15 real outline units from two real applications plus 22 synthetic units (traps, number bait, thin evidence, adjacent skills, conviction paragraphs). The cases and results live in the gitignored `.claude/hillclimb/variant-generation/`, because they hold his real résumé and job descriptions.

The first pilot measured nothing, and that was the most important finding. The bundled Claude CLI now runs Agent-tool subagents in the background. The variant client dispatched `conjurer:variant-generator`, got "Async agent launched", and ended its turn. The parent model then invented a "[notification received]" line and a "verbatim" variant block. It had not read the grimoire or the résumé, and its format did not parse, so live generation produced zero variants per unit. `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_BACKGROUND_TASKS=1` in the client env restores synchronous dispatch; a single-unit probe went from 0 to 4 grounded variants, and the live adapter test passes. The runner had scored the empty generations as plausible all-zero rows. It now sends zero-variant attempts to `errors.jsonl` as `unparseable_output`. A zero that hides a crash is worse than a crash.

The Agent SDK loads every user-level MCP server's tool schemas even with `setting_sources=[]` and `tools=[]`. One minimal judge call wrote 188,855 cache-creation tokens on Opus 5.5 ($1.52); `strict_mcp_config=True` removed them ($0.10). Production's variant client has the same exposure, which also widens the prompt-injection surface around a hostile pasted JD (issue #35).

Jev questions share their request's state, and unrelated state lowers jev-1.13 accuracy. So each variant gets one request per distinct state: cited lines, evidence pool, claim alone. An adoption question that asked whether the evidence "documents" use judged the strength of his sources rather than whether the claim exceeds them, and put a verbatim résumé line at 0.57. Rephrasing it relative to the evidence lines fixed that: 9/9 known-good and known-bad claims pass. The three overclaim nouls fire together on any overclaim, so only their combined metric is trustworthy.

The Opus pairwise judge agreed with his real picks on 31 and then 32 of 45 pairs, with no position bias. All the first-round misses cited restraint or overclaiming. The judge cannot see evidence, so it called a sourced adoption claim an overclaim; restraint now belongs to Jev alone. The remaining disagreement is close calls among four good variants, so the judge is a secondary, tiebreak signal by his decision.

Cover paragraphs mix facts and opinion, so Jev reads their citations as partly supporting and `verdict_for` marks every one `adds_detail`. Cover units now pass `fact_clean` on no unstated fact, no contradiction, and no unsourced digit. Word-number matching misfires in prose both ways ("those three primitives" flagged; "fourteen-plus loops" missed).

Open: the Jev pool request sends the full master résumé, including the contact line, on every variant, as production `verify.py` does. Trimming non-claim lines would match the grimoire's "only approved, necessary excerpts" rule.
